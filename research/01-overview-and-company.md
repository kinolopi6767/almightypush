# 01 — Overview & Company

## What LaraPush Is

LaraPush is a **self-hosted push notification panel** (not a SaaS). Customers pay a one-time fee for a **perpetual license**, install the panel on their own server, and then:

- Register **unlimited domains/websites**
- Collect **unlimited subscribers**
- Send **unlimited campaigns** for an unlimited period
- Keep all subscriber tokens on **their own server** (privacy/security selling point)

Delivery is powered by **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)** — customers only need a (free) Google account. Claimed sending speed up to **1.5 million notifications per minute** (marketed as "5x faster than other push services").

Tagline: *"Say 'NO' to MONTHLY EXPENSES for Sending Push Notifications"* / *"Pay Once, Use Forever"*.

## Product Positioning

- Unique selling point: **Lifetime deal (LTD)** — no recurring monthly bills, unlike OneSignal, iZooto, TruePush, PushEngage, etc.
- Target audience: bloggers, media/news sites, digital publishers, e-commerce sites, media houses, app developers.
- Claims: customers with **30M+ subscribers** on the $750 (Pro) tier; some "more than 400 million subscribers"; panel tested on **150M+ tokens**.
- Anti-SaaS message: SaaS providers charge per subscriber/domain and can inject ads; LaraPush stores tokens on your server so "nobody can use them unless you allow."

## Origin Story

- Team of bloggers frustrated with recurring fees ("hundreds and sometimes thousands of dollars every month").
- Founded **January 2021** by **Anirudh Saraya (Co-founder)**.
- Started at $99 (early M/L pricing), later evolved through paid major-version upgrades (v2 → v3 → v4 → v5 currently being sold as "Startup v5" / "Pro v5" at $499/$799).
