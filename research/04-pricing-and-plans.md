## License Model (from Terms)
- **Perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable** license for **one server** owned/managed by you.
- Lifetime usage of the purchased version; **free updates within the same major version** (e.g., 2.0 → 2.x).
- **Paid upgrades across major versions** (e.g., 2 → 3 → 4 → 5).
- License key delivered by email; entered at panel registration; required on updates.
- Support is **not** lifetime: **6 months free support (Startup), 12 months (Pro)**; additional support purchasable with upgrades.
- No refunds after purchase (except exceptional cases within 3 days); "powered by" watermark must not be removed.

## Value Proposition Math (as marketed)
- "Other services charge $3,000+ for 1M subscribers; LaraPush customers with 30M+ subscribers pay only $750 [Pro at old price]."
- SaaS comparison: typical marketer pays $50–$1,000/month on push notification services.
- Cost-avoidance example: 0.5M subscribers/year would cost >$5,000/year on SaaS alternatives.
